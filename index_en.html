FilmScan Pro

Real-time film negative preview and mask removal

How to use:

  • Place the film on an evenly lit screen or light box.
  • Aim at the film base, then tap “Sample Mask” to remove the negative’s color cast.
  • Adjust the controls for the best preview.

⚠ Note: Phone white-balance processing and the backlight source affect color. This tool is intended for real-time preview only and cannot match the color accuracy of a professional scanner.
